# Pawfolio
COMP 34125 F2026

Pawfolio is an ASP.NET web application designed to help pet parents conveniently manage and organize their pets’ health information in one centralized platform.

Pet health information such as weight changes, vaccinations, medications, allergies, symptoms, and health progress is often scattered across paper records, photos, or different veterinary clinics. Pawfolio addresses this issue by providing a user-friendly system where pet parents can record, monitor, and maintain accurate and up-to-date health records.

Key features of Pawfolio will include:

Weight tracking

Vaccine and medication history

Allergy information

Symptom tracking

Health progress tracking, including measurements and photos

Compilation of pet health records

Downloadable health records in PDF, DOC, or TXT formats

Vet escalation capabilities for situations requiring professional attention

The application is intended to make pet health information easier to maintain, monitor, and share with veterinary professionals. By keeping important records organized in one location, Pawfolio can support better communication with veterinarians and help pet parents maintain continuous and informed care for their pets.
